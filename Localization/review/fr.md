# fr: review

A careful, idiomatic French translation that uses vous throughout. It uses the Ameli/HAS patient terms (bouffées de chaleur, sueurs nocturnes, THM/THS, progestatif, saignements après la ménopause, insuffisance ovarienne prématurée, sécheresse vaginale), keeps vague / Veille de nuit / bilan du soir / tendance / essai / canicule perso consistent across the UI, widgets, Watch, Siri phrases and store, uses Apple's official French terms (Santé, écran verrouillé, centre de contrôle, bouton Action, Concentration, Toucher deux fois, Digital Crown), and applies clean French typography (non-breaking spaces before : ; ? %, « » with inner spaces, curly apostrophes, no exclamation marks). Every safety string and article is faithful and complete with all hedges intact. The changes are small: a scale label that read too strong (Notable → Perceptible), placeholders whose grammar broke with real values (Début de %@, Rendez-vous %@), one unidiomatic emergency instruction, a few calques, a typography fix, and a stronger keyword set.

## Residual doubts

- Brain fog (#131, #342): « brouillard mental » is the more common term in France, but at 17 characters it exceeds the 16-character label budget of #131. « Brume mentale » is understood (and pairs well with the anchors « Esprit clair » ↔ « Brouillard épais »), so it is kept everywhere for consistency. If the #131 budget can grow to 17, switch both #131 and #342 to « Brouillard mental ».
- Short « Bouffée » / « Bouffées » labels (#295, #298, max 16) read naturally as everyday shorthand for « bouffée de chaleur » next to « Sueur nocturne »; the full term is used wherever space allows. Kept.
- #551 « Cœur qui bat vite, fort ou de façon irrégulière » mirrors an Apple Health symptom name; Apple's exact French wording in Santé could not be checked offline. Kept.
- Subtitle « Journal de bouffées de chaleur » (30/30) drops the clinician-notes half of the English. There is no room for both at 30 characters, and « bouffées de chaleur » is the strongest French search term, so it is kept. « médecin » is in the keywords.
