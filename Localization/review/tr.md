# tr: review

A strong, careful Turkish translation. Medical terms follow T.C. Sağlık Bakanlığı public usage (sıcak basması, gece terlemesi, menopoz sonrası kanama, hormon tedavisi, progestojen, vajinal kuruluk, çarpıntı), every safety string is faithful and complete with hedges intact, and the known bleeding trap is avoided: 'menopoz sonrası' is the standard Turkish term and reads as after periods have stopped, with the article spelling out the 12-month rule. The hardest Turkish problem, suffixes on inserted values, is handled well: no case suffix is ever attached to a placeholder (label: value, 'saat %@', 'ile', '%@ saatine kadar', '%@ numarasını ara'), percentages use the Turkish %42 order, and every apostrophe suffix on fixed names (MenoMap'a/'ı/'ın/'ta/'tan, iPhone'da/'unda, Watch'tan, PDF'i, 19:00'dan, 3'te, dk'dan) follows vowel harmony and consonant assimilation. Register 'sen' is kept: it is the norm for Turkish consumer wellness and cycle apps (Meditopia, Flo, Clue), held consistently, and questions addressed to the doctor correctly switch to 'siz'; not changed. Fixes are few: an ambiguous sentence in the stages article, a clearer safety question about logged bleeding, a stiff PDF sentence, the terse 'At' for Discard, two wording fixes (Sunday wrap toggle, tonight's heads-up card) and a more natural first store screenshot.

## Residual doubts

- Register: kept 'sen'. Apple's own Turkish UI and some public-sector health services use 'siz', and a 45–60 audience may find 'siz' more respectful; but Turkish consumer wellness/cycle apps overwhelmingly use 'sen' and the translation holds it consistently, so a global switch was not justified.
- Tab 'You' = 'Sen' (and paths 'Sen → Gizlilik'): unusual as a tab label but parallels YouTube's Turkish 'Siz' tab; 'Profil' would be an alternative if user testing shows confusion (would require updating ids 291, 348, 479, 742, 815, 821).
- Glossary lists 'menopoz sonrası kanama (adetler kesildikten sonra)' but the parenthetical is not used in UI strings; 'menopoz sonrası' is the standard Sağlık Bakanlığı term and the article states the 12-month rule, so no addition was made to safety strings.
