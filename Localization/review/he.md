Safety strings and hedges are complete. The main systematic problem was bidi: about fifteen strings and paragraphs (several safety ones, the Info.plist Health texts and the store description) began with 'MenoMap' or 'PDF', which makes iOS lay the paragraph out left-to-right; these now open with a Hebrew word (היישום MenoMap…, קובץ PDF…), and the '+' sign of the wrist-temperature delta is anchored with an LRM. Beyond that, a handful of meaning slips (a button subtitle in the past tense, 'log in a few days', 'in a short queue', 'מרגוע'), two ungrammatical sentences, experiment results that said 'the period' instead of the experiment, and one flat wry line.

- id 806 relies on an invisible U+200E before %1$@ so '+0.4°' renders correctly in RTL; if a future tool strips invisible characters the sign will again appear after the number. A code-side fix (format the signed number with the locale, or wrap it in an LTR isolate) would be sturdier.
