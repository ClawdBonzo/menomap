# nl: review

A careful, faithful Dutch translation: Thuisarts.nl patient terms (opvlieger, nachtelijk zweten, overgang, bloedverlies na de overgang, hormoontherapie, progestageen), consistent je-address, Apple's official Dutch UI terms (toegangsscherm, bedieningspaneel, actieknop, Opdrachten, Gezondheid-app, Delen met gezin) and a steady glossary (golf, Nachtwacht, privézomer, Hitterapport, Afspraakverslag) across UI, widgets, Watch, Siri phrases and store. Changes are limited to a few safety strings whose grammar blurred the message (713, 715, 403), one misspelt medical term and ambiguity in the menopause article, calques ('Privé vanaf de basis', 'ik loop warm', 'Zie patronen'), and the Belgian-friendly 'noodnummer'; all hedges, placeholders and plurals were intact.

Changed 21 of about 877 entries (2%).

## Residual doubts

- 327 'Ik lig te zweten' (I'm having a night sweat) drops the word 'nacht' but reads naturally for a bedtime button; kept.
- 756 'Typisch' as a stat label can also mean 'odd' in Dutch; kept because the duration value next to it makes the sense clear.
- d2 'Dr. De Vries': Dutch style writes 'dr.' in lower case mid-sentence; capitalised here as a stand-alone label, which is common in both NL and BE.
