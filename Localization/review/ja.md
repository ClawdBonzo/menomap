# ja: review

A careful, fluent translation: です・ます is consistent, patient terms match Japanese public-health usage (ホットフラッシュ, 寝汗, 閉経後の出血, ホルモン補充療法, 黄体ホルモン, 腟, 血管運動神経症状), every safety string is faithful and complete with all hedges intact, full-width punctuation is clean, and the glossary (ほてり, 夜の見守り, ふり返り, 受診メモ, ヘルスケア) holds across UI, widgets, Watch, Siri phrases and store. The wry lines (私だけ真夏, 丑三つ時クラブ, 体内サウナ：稼働中, 今はムリ。溶けてます。) are gentle and would make a Japanese woman in her 50s smile, so they stay. Changes fix one real comprehension risk (the perimenopause stage label 閉経前後 overlapping with 閉経後 in the stage picker and article), a handful of stiff or calqued lines, Watch breathing-phase consistency, one Siri phrase, an Apple term, and store keywords.
