# sv: review

Good, natural Swedish: du-register and gender-neutral wording held throughout; 1177.se terms used consistently (värmevallningar, nattsvettningar, klimakteriet/menopaus, hormonbehandling/MHT, gestagen, stänkblödning, torrhet i slidan); glossary (våg, Nattvakt, Värmerapport, Veckan som gick, privat sommar) consistent across UI, widgets, Watch, Siri phrases and store; all max budgets, placeholders and plurals correct; articles keep their markdown and every hedge. Known trap checked: every bleeding string and the article say 'efter menopausen' (after the final period), never 'efter klimakteriet'. Apple terms verified against support.apple.com/sv-se: 'snabbknappen' (Action Button, confirmed in the live sv-se page 'Använd och anpassa snabbknappen på iPhone'), låsskärmen, hemskärmen, Kontrollcenter, liveaktiviteter, Standby, Genvägar, appen Hälsa. Fixes: 'samverkar med läkemedel' (reads as 'cooperate') replaced with 'interagerar' in a safety answer and the non-hormonal article; one safety line strengthened 'often' to 'usually' (fixed); neuter agreement for medication/experiment status labels; id 636 made a verb button; a past-tense 'i morse' in a morning heads-up; two small consistency/naturalness fixes. Extras reviewed: store fits all limits, keeps both URLs and the full disclaimer; demo data is local and plausible; no changes needed.
