# nb: review

A solid, natural Bokmål translation: consistent du-address, helsenorge.no patient terms (hetetokt, nattesvette, hormonbehandling, gestagen, hjertebank, tørrhet i skjeden), a steady glossary (bølge, Nattevakt, privat hetebølge, Heterapport, Uka som gikk, Legetimerapport) and Apple's Norwegian platform terms (låst skjerm, Kontrollsenter, handlingsknappen, Oppdatering i sanntid – checked on support.apple.com/no-no, Helse-appen, Snarveier). The uncountable-nattesvette workaround holds up: counted forms use 'episoder med nattesvette' and mixed counts use label: value, with no broken agreement. The one systematic problem was the known trap: every 'bleeding after menopause' string used 'etter overgangsalderen' (after the transition years) and was changed to 'etter menopausen' in the UI, PDF, disclaimer and article. Other fixes: the setup Siri tip quoted a non-existent phrase, a few calques and clumsy constructions (Best verdi, Privat fra grunnen av, fortsatt for 'continued', natten i natt, double 'fra'), and two ambiguous labels (Tracking header, Peak). Hedges, safety wording, placeholders and plural forms were otherwise intact.

## Residual doubts

- 'Blødning etter overgangsalderen' is common in Norwegian lay sources (NHI); switched to 'etter menopausen' per the brief's known trap. Article 42 defines menopause as the final period, so the term is explained in-app.
- Id 70 singular form 'I 1 syklus registrerte du …' is understandable but stiff; kept because every plural form must carry the placeholder.
- Strings 473/474 ('På %lld dager …', 'Etter %lld kvelder …') assume counts above 1, as the English does.
