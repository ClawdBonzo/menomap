# da: review

A careful, faithful Danish translation in consistent du-register using sundhed.dk patient terms (hedeture, nattesved, overgangsalderen, hormonbehandling, gestagen, tørhed i skeden, hjertebanken) and a steady glossary (bølge, Nattevagt, privat hedebølge, Varmerapport, aftentjek, Besøgsrapport) across UI, widgets, Watch, Siri phrases, PDF and store. Every bleeding string uses 'blødning efter menopausen' (after periods have stopped), so the known 'overgangsalder' trap is avoided; all safety strings, hedges, placeholders and plurals are intact, and the many-number insight strings were sensibly recast as label: value. Apple terms checked against support.apple.com/da-dk (Kontrolcenter, Handlingsknappen, låseskærm, betjeningsmuligheder). Fixes are limited to check-in scale labels that misfire in Danish ('Fuld' = drunk, 'Lav' mood, 'I top' stress), a few calques ('Store retningslinjer', 'Privat fra bunden', 'Samtale'), an ambiguous tab reference, an imperative used as a time label, one broken duration sentence and small consistency points.

- Id 682/690/691: 'betjeningsmulighed(er)' matches Apple's Danish guide heading 'Tilpas betjeningsmuligheder'; the exact button labels 'Vælg en betjeningsmulighed'/'Tilføj en betjeningsmulighed' could not be seen in the rendered guide.
- 'Apple Sundhed' as a standalone label: Apple's Danish app name is 'Sundhed' (used as 'appen Sundhed' in running text, as the translation does); kept 'Apple Sundhed' where the English says 'Apple Health' as a source label.
- Demo d2 'Læge Hansen': Danes rarely use a title with a surname; acceptable for screenshots, kept.
