# es-419: review

Strong, natural neutral Latin American Spanish: consistent tú, no vosotros or Spain-only vocabulary, LatAm patient terms (bochornos, sudores nocturnos, terapia hormonal, sangrado después de la menopausia) and Apple es-419 platform terms throughout, with safety strings faithful and complete and glossary terms (oleada, Guardia Nocturna, repaso, app Salud) held across UI, widgets, Watch, Siri and store. Changes were small: time placeholders preceded by 'las' (wrong for 1 o'clock) recast as labels, one safety-article drift ('ligero' added to a colour description), a Siri-hint string quoting a phrase that isn't localized, a few calques/grammar slips ('mi persona', 'si podrías', '¿Dónde estás?' for stage) and minor store polish.
