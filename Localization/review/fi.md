## Residual doubts

- Siri phrases (108, 222, 368, 369) use '${applicationName}-apissa/-appiin'. This is natural spoken Finnish and keeps the token intact, but whether Siri's matcher treats the hyphenated suffix as a separate word could only be confirmed on a Finnish-language device.
- 'Ohjauskeskus' (Control Center) did not appear in the fi-fi guide TOC excerpt checked; it is the long-standing Finnish iOS term and was kept.
- IAP names ('MenoMap Pro kuukausi/vuosi') are lowercase period words after the brand; acceptable, 'MenoMap Pro, kuukausi' would be an alternative if ASC review prefers.
