# pl: review

A strong, careful Polish translation: calm patient-leaflet register with consistent informal 'ty' and feminine forms, correct pacjent.gov.pl-style medical terms (uderzenia gorąca, nocne poty, krwawienie po menopauzie meaning after periods have stopped, progestagen), faithful safety strings and articles with markdown intact, and correct one/few/many/other plurals throughout. Fixes were needed in four areas: Apple platform terms (Apple's Polish guides use 'ekran główny' for Home Screen and 'narzędzia'/'Dodaj narzędzie' for Controls, not 'ekran początkowy'/'elementy sterujące'; 'wydarzenia na żywo' is confirmed and lowercased in running text); the HRT abbreviation (Polish consumer sources overwhelmingly use HTZ, so HTZ is now the UI term and the article names both); captions shown under a large number (genitive plural 'fal'/'upałów' broke for 2-4, now nominative caption forms); and a few clunky or ungrammatical lines ('%@ trwa' with the plural 'Nocne poty', the medication safety line, a doubled colon, a repetitive store sentence).

Changed 21 of about 877 entries (2%).
