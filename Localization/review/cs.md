# cs: review

Very good, natural Czech in a consistent ty-register with feminine forms, patient-leaflet terms matching NZIP (návaly horka, noční pocení, menopauza/perimenopauza/postmenopauza, hormonální léčba, gestagen, suchost pochvy, bušení srdce). Plurals are correct in all four CLDR categories, with 'many' (decimals) using the genitive singular (1,5 dne, vlny, návalu horka, noci). Every bleeding string uses 'po menopauze' and the article defines it as 12 months without a period, so the known trap is avoided. Hedges and safety strings are faithful. Apple terms were checked against Apple CZ support: zamknutá obrazovka, Ovládací centrum, akční tlačítko and živé aktivity are correct, but Double Tap is 'dvojí sepnutí prstů', not 'poklepání'. Fixes: that Apple term, a price placeholder that could not agree with 's' (Pokračovat za %@), a VoiceOver percent string that broke for 1–4 (now label form), one safety line where 'often' became 'usually', 'premature ovarian insufficiency', the trial-eligibility sentence, and a handful of calques or awkward word order.

Changed 14 of about 877 entries (1%).

## Residual doubts

- Share-card captions under big numbers (433 'zaznamenaných nočních vln', 672 'zaznamenaných vln', 523, 671, 670) use the genitive plural, which is right for 0 and 5+ and the usual Czech infographic style, but reads oddly under 1–4. Left as is because monthly and two-week counts are usually 5+.
- 774 'Budíček' for Wake: Apple's Clock/Sleep uses 'Večerka' for Bedtime (as used here); the Wake label may be 'Buzení' in Apple CZ. Left 'Budíček' because it is clear either way.
