## 1. What MenoMap is, and what it isn't

MenoMap is a wellness tracker and education tool. It helps you record hot flashes, night sweats, sleep, mood,
medications and other things you notice, see them over time, and summarize them for a clinician.

MenoMap is not a medical device. It does not diagnose menopause, perimenopause or any other condition, does not
prescribe, recommend, start, stop or change any treatment, and does not replace care from a qualified clinician.
Patterns in MenoMap compare your own entries: they are coincidence checks, not proof that one thing causes another.
Always talk to a clinician before making decisions about your health or your medicines.

## 2. Urgent symptoms

MenoMap cannot assess symptoms or emergencies and does not monitor your entries for you. Bleeding after menopause
(even once, even light) should be checked by a clinician. Chest pain, a sudden severe headache, weakness on one side,
trouble breathing or fainting need emergency care: contact your local emergency number.
