**Subject:** For World Menopause Day: the night sweat button that's there at 3am

Hi [Name],

For World Menopause Day on October 18: many women in perimenopause say the hardest part of their appointment is
remembering. How many hot flashes last week? How bad were the nights? They walk in with a vague "it's been rough."

MenoMap, a new iPhone and Apple Watch app, takes a different approach to menopause tracking:

- **One tap, even at 3am.** A night-sweat button stays on the Lock Screen all night ("Night Watch"), or on Apple Watch,
  so logging doesn't mean unlocking a bright phone.
- **Notes a clinician can read.** Before a visit, the app makes a one-page PDF: frequency, intensity, sleep and what
  changed, in any of 34 languages.
- **Private by design.** No account, no cloud, no ads. The data never leaves the iPhone, which matters now that
  reproductive and health data privacy is in the news.
- **Works with Apple Health's new menopause features in iOS 27.**

For World Menopause Day it runs "Two Weeks of Night Watch": track two weeks of nights, then bring the page to your
appointment.

I'm happy to answer questions, share screenshots or a sample clinician PDF, or set up free Pro access for you to try.
